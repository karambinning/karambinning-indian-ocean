— The kitchen

A quiet table,
a long evening,
and the real thing.

Indian Ocean is a small Indian restaurant on Swan Street, a short walk from Loughborough Market Place. The kitchen cooks the cuisine it knows best — traditional, carefully spiced, made to order from fresh ingredients. Nothing is rushed; nothing is cut.

We are a bring your own house. Carry in whatever you would like to drink — a bottle of red, a few lagers, a Riesling for the prawn dishes — and we will pour it for you at the table.

For the full menu, please call ahead, or step in and ask. The card is large, the changes are small, and the favourites have been the favourites for some time.

  • Tandoori charcoal clay
  • Curries house & chef’s
  • Biryanis slow-cooked
  • Breads fresh, hot
  • Vegetarianfull section
  • Sundries rice & relish

— Visit

A short walk from
Market Place, on Swan Street.

The restaurant sits on the quieter end of Swan Street, between the Market Place and the Rushes. Park in the Carillon Court car park, the Rushes, or anywhere on-street after six — most of the meters are off by the time the kitchen opens.

Tables fill up on Friday and Saturday. A short ring ahead is always worth it.
